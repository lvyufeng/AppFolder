AppFolder 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

中文 · English

生效日期：2026-10-13　最后更新：2026-10-30

中文

AppFolder 不向开发者上传个人数据，不设账号，不使用广告、追踪、第三方统计或崩溃上报 SDK。开发者不运营用于接收 App 使用数据的服务器。

设备上的数据：小组件的动作选择和外观配置由 iOS WidgetKit 管理。你在「我的分组」中保存的分组名称、绑定动作名称、自定义显示名称、文件夹树、排列顺序、空位、分组及子文件夹外观（网格、布局密度、名称显示、图标大小、底板、颜色、返回按钮样式、自定间距、四周边距以及文字颜色与描边）、每个文件夹的内容缩略图网格会保存在 App 与小组件的本地 App Group 共享容器中，用于呈现布局并按名称匹配已配置的动作；分组文件不保存系统动作本身。文件夹封面、网站图标和联系人图标的普通及深色图标配置（照片小图、符号、文字或日期样式）保存在同一 App Group 容器的独立封面资源区 FolderCoverAssets，分组只保存资源引用；未保存的新封面、网站图标和联系人图标暂存在本地 FolderCoverDrafts 草稿区。封面编辑历史只在本次编辑中保留，取消整个分组会丢弃未提交的封面草稿。为显示和修正图标，AppFolder 会在 App 与小组件的共享容器中保存图标缓存、名称与商店编号及所选商店地区的匹配记录（用于后续重新获取图标）、你指定的图标配置（包括选中的照片小图、符号、文字、日期样式和背景色），以及小组件上报的名称和图标记录。搜索地区偏好只保存在这台设备上，不进入备份，也不会改变 Apple 账户地区或系统动作。还会保存文件夹路径、展开页码等显示状态；这些内容不会上传给开发者。开发调试版本可能保存最多约 256 KB 的本地诊断日志；App Store 发布版本不会写入此日志。

动作资料库：你主动保存的动作名称、标签和归档状态保存在本地 App Group 共享容器中，不上传给开发者。列表会合并小组件历史记录、已保存分组中的动作名、图标或商店匹配记录和手工记录，不代表已安装 App 清单或当前可运行的系统动作。整理、筛选和归档本身不联网；归档只隐藏默认列表中的条目，不删除分组节点、图标或系统动作。全库备份包含全部已保存资料库记录，包括未加入分组及已归档的记录；单分组分享仅带该组动作名称对应的资料库记录。导入保留本地已有同名资料库记录，不合并或覆盖其标签和归档状态。

外观预设：你主动命名保存的外观预设保存在本地 App Group 共享容器中，最多 64 条，只包含名称和布局、图标大小、名称显示、底板、颜色、返回按钮样式、自定间距、四周边距以及文字颜色与描边等外观选项，不含分组内容或照片。保存、改名或移除预设不会修改已经使用该外观的分组；只有主动应用预设才更新当前分组或子文件夹的编辑草稿，并可在本次编辑中撤销。完整备份包含预设；单分组分享只携带该组已保存的外观，不带全局预设库。导入保留本地已有同名预设，不自动应用预设。预设管理不联网，不上传给开发者。

日期图标：日期图标仅保存样式类型和背景色，不保存某一天的日期或图片快照。月份与日号在显示时按设备当前日历、语言与地区设置和时区生成；这些显示设置不写入图标文件或备份。AppFolder 不读取日历事件，也不申请日历访问权限；此功能不联网，不向开发者发送数据。小组件的实际刷新由系统安排，日期或时区变化后可能有延迟。

配置检查：配置检查在本地 App Group 共享容器中保留最近最多 24 份刷新报告，包括分组标识、布局版本、配置标识、动作名称及匹配状态、网格、组件尺寸和刷新时间。报告不保存 SystemShortcut 等系统动作对象，不上传给开发者，也不写入导出的备份。它只说明最近刷新时的名称匹配结果，不能证明小组件现在仍在桌面；相同配置的多个小组件共用一份记录。报告不是桌面小组件实例清单。新增报告超过上限时会淘汰较旧记录；删除分组只移除保存的布局，不会立即清除其历史检查报告。

联网：搜索与显示图标时，AppFolder 会向 Apple 的 iTunes Search / Lookup 接口（itunes.apple.com）发送搜索词、App 名称或商店编号，以及请求所用的商店区域参数，并从接口提供的图像地址下载图标（通常为 Apple 的 mzstatic.com）。标准网络连接会向服务端提供 IP 地址等连接信息。AppFolder 不额外发送广告标识符、账号或精确位置。Apple 对其服务的处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。

网站链接：网站名称、完整 HTTP/HTTPS 链接（包括查询参数和片段）及每个网站的打开方式保存在本地分组中。「网站打开设置」中的统一偏好只保存在这台设备上，仅用于明确选择「跟随 App 设置」的网站；未设置打开方式的旧网站和新建网站默认仍使用系统默认方式。更改统一偏好不会改写保存的网址、访问网络或检查已安装的浏览器。输入或编辑网址、选择打开方式、添加或保存链接、布局预览、分享扩展接收和备份导入都不会自行访问网站或下载图标。点击「测试链接」或网站图块时，按你选择的方式打开完整链接；默认方式由系统处理，桌面默认网站图块无需经过 AppFolder 中转。选择「App 内浏览」、Chrome、Firefox 或「跟随 App 设置」的桌面网站会先进入 AppFolder，再交给所选浏览方式；「跟随 App 设置」在打开时使用本机当前统一偏好。App 内浏览使用系统提供的 Safari 模态页面，浏览时可加载网站资源并执行脚本；AppFolder 不读取该 Safari 页面的内容、密码或浏览记录。网站及浏览器按各自的政策处理数据。无法打开所选浏览器时会提示，不会自动换用其他浏览器；你可以主动选择系统默认方式。网站名称、链接、每网站的打开方式（包括跟随选择）和已保存图标会包含在你主动导出或分享的分组文件中；本机统一偏好不导出。这些数据不会发送给开发者。

获取网站信息：只有在网站编辑器中主动点击「获取网站信息」时，AppFolder 才会访问输入的网站、经验证的 HTTP/HTTPS 重定向地址，以及该网站声明的图标服务器或 CDN；未找到可用图标时，会尝试该站点的 /favicon.ico。此元数据获取功能只读取有限的 HTML 内容来提取名称和图标地址，不将网页正文作为分组内容保存。此获取过程不使用第三方网站图标查询服务，不渲染网页或执行脚本，不使用浏览器的登录状态，也不持久保存 Cookie、网页响应缓存或登录凭据。网站及图标服务器会收到请求网址（包括其中的查询参数）、IP 地址等正常网络信息，并按其自身政策处理请求；这些信息不会上传给开发者。获取的名称和图标先作为候选显示，只有你选择应用并保存分组后，才成为本地网站名称和独立图标；获取过程不会用重定向地址或网页声明的规范地址改写你保存的目标网址。

联系人图块：你可以手动输入姓名和号码，或在系统联系人选择器中主动选择联系人及一个电话号码。AppFolder 只接收最终选中的姓名与号码，不读取整个通讯录、联系人照片或持久联系人标识。姓名、单个号码、所选操作和自定义图标保存在本地分组中，不会随通讯录自动更新，也不会上传给开发者。选择、预览、保存和导入不会发起联系。点击图块后由 AppFolder 读取当前记录，再请求系统处理电话或短信；电话由系统确认，短信不会自动发送。FaceTime 必须在 App 内再次点击开始；排队、过期或恢复的请求不会自动执行。姓名、号码、操作和相关图像会包含在你主动导出或分享的分组文件中。

共享收件箱：只有在系统分享面板中选择 AppFolder 并点击「保存」后，完整网址（含查询参数和片段）、手动填写的可选名称、记录标识和时间才会保存在本地 App Group 容器的 WebShareInbox 中，最多保留 100 条。此分享接收过程不读取分享正文或剪贴板，不获取网页内容或自动提取标题、图标等元数据，不联网，也不会自动启动主 App。在加入分组流程中，只有你确认并成功保存分组后，App 才自动移除对应待添加记录；取消添加或保存失败时保留。你也可以在「待添加网页」中左滑主动移除单条记录，并在当前页面撤销最近一次移除；这不会删除已保存分组中的内容。未加入分组的待添加记录不包含在备份或「分享分组」文件中。

预览：App 内的示例预览使用本地示例图形，不读取你的桌面、通知、壁纸或已安装 App 列表，也不联网。

权限：AppFolder 不请求整个通讯录的访问权限，也不请求日历、位置、麦克风、相机或通知权限，不枚举已安装 App。动作图标、联系人图标、网站图标和文件夹封面的图片可通过系统照片选择器或文件选择器导入，只读取你明确选中的图片，不获取整个照片库的访问权。选择 iCloud Drive 或其他云端位置时，系统或相应文件服务提供商可能下载该文件。AppFolder 不保存原始文件、源 URL 或持续访问授权；图片会在设备上裁剪并重新编码为小图，不保留原始照片的定位等元数据，也不会上传给开发者。你在系统小组件编辑面板中自行选择动作。

备份文件：你可以主动导出包含分组布局、联系人姓名和单个号码及操作、网站名称、完整链接及打开方式（格式版本 13 起；版本 14 新增跟随 App 设置选择）、普通及深色文件夹封面、自定义图标（包括联系人和网站图标、照片小图及日期样式配置）和手动图标匹配（含所选商店地区）的备份文件。照片封面、联系人图标和网站图标也会包含在备份中；多处使用的同一图像只保存一份。「分享分组」生成仅包含所选已保存分组及其关联图像、匹配和动作资料库记录的本地临时文件，由你在系统分享面板中选择接收方或保存位置。当前导出格式为版本 16，可读取版本 1–16。备份不包含系统动作、桌面小组件配置、搜索地区偏好、本机网站统一打开偏好、下载缓存、配置检查报告或诊断日志。导入只读取你选择的文件，并在确认后新增分组副本及缺失的图标设置；已有自定义图标和手动匹配会保留。导入不会恢复系统动作选择，动作仍需在系统小组件面板中重新配置；网站不需要配置系统动作；旧备份没有打开方式字段时使用系统默认方式；明确保存的系统默认方式也保持不变。导入会保留每个网站的跟随选择，之后打开时使用目标设备的统一偏好。导入不会打开网站或联网，也不会改写保存的网址。备份文件未由 AppFolder 加密；它保存到你在系统文件面板选择的位置。如果你选择 iCloud Drive 或其他云端位置，文件由相应服务提供商处理，不会发送给开发者。删除 App 内的数据不会删除已经导出的备份副本，你可以在“文件”中管理或删除这些副本。

保留与删除：在动作资料库中移除记录并保存整理，会清除该名称的资料库标签和归档记录，不删除分组或图标；其他来源仍提供这个名称时，它会继续出现在列表中。分组布局、图标与匹配记录保存在设备上，供后续显示使用。你可以在「我的分组」中删除分组，移除该分组已保存的布局。你可以在文件夹菜单的「编辑封面」中将普通设为「内容缩略图」、深色设为「跟随普通封面」，保存分组后移除该文件夹的自定义封面设置；也可以删除文件夹或分组。网站菜单可以编辑或删除链接，也可以恢复默认网站图标；保存分组后生效。仍被其他分组、当前编辑历史或删除撤销记录引用的封面资源会保留，不再使用的资源会在后续本地清理时移除。你可以在「图标工作室」中选择「全部恢复自动图标」，移除该动作名称的手动指定和普通、深色自定义图标；这不会清除所有下载缓存。删除 App（而非仅卸载以保留数据）会移除 App 的本地数据；共享容器的清理由 iOS 管理。

App Store 统计：如果 Apple 提供汇总下载或诊断报告，开发者可能查看这些报告。AppFolder 不内置额外分析或崩溃上报服务。

儿童与变更：AppFolder 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，不会有意收集儿童信息。政策变更会在本页发布，并更新日期。

English

AppFolder does not upload personal data to the developer. It has no accounts and uses no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives app usage data.

On-device data. iOS WidgetKit manages the widget's selected actions and appearance. Group names, linked action names, custom display names, folder trees, ordering, empty cells, group and subfolder appearance (grid, layout density, label visibility, icon size, plate, color, back-button style, custom spacing and margins, and title color and outline), and each folder’s contents-preview grid saved in My Groups are stored in the app and widget's local App Group container to display layouts and match configured actions by name. Group files do not store the system actions themselves. Standard and dark artwork settings for folder covers, website icons, and contact icons (photo thumbnails, symbols, text, or date styles) are kept separately in FolderCoverAssets within the same App Group container; groups store only references. New, unsaved covers, website icons, and contact icons stay in the local FolderCoverDrafts staging area. Cover edit history lasts only for the current editing session; discarding the whole group draft removes its uncommitted cover drafts. To display and correct icons, AppFolder stores artwork, app-name-to-store-ID matches and their selected storefront countries (used to fetch icons again), your icon overrides (including selected photo thumbnails, symbols, text, date styles, and background colors), and names and icon records reported by the widget in a shared on-device container. The search-region preference stays only on this device and is not included in backups. It does not change the Apple Account region or system actions. AppFolder also stores display state such as folder paths and expanded pages. This data is not uploaded to the developer. Development builds may keep a local diagnostic log of approximately 256 KB; App Store release builds do not write this log.

Action library. Action names, tags, and archive states that you choose to save are stored in the local App Group container and are not uploaded to the developer. The list combines past widget reports, action names in saved groups, icon or store-match records, and manual records; it is not an inventory of installed apps or currently runnable system actions. Organizing, filtering, and archiving make no network requests. Archiving hides an entry from the default list without removing group tiles, icons, or system actions. Full backups include all saved library records, including unused and archived entries. Share Group includes only library records matching that group's action names. Import preserves existing local records with the same name rather than merging or overwriting their tags or archive states.

Appearance presets. Named appearance presets you choose to save are stored in the local App Group container, with a limit of 64. They contain a name and appearance choices such as grid, density, icon size, label visibility, background, color, back-button style, custom spacing and margins, and title color and outline, without group contents or photos. Saving, renaming, or removing a preset does not change groups that previously used it. Explicitly applying a preset updates only the current group or folder draft and can be undone during that editing session. Full backups include presets; Share Group includes the saved appearance of that group without the global preset library. Import preserves existing local presets with the same name and does not apply presets automatically. Preset management does not access the network or send data to the developer.

Date icons. Date icons save only the style type and background color, not a fixed date or image snapshot. The month and day are generated using the device’s current calendar, locale, and time zone; these display settings are not embedded in icon files or backups. AppFolder does not read calendar events or request calendar access. This feature makes no network requests and sends no data to the developer. The system schedules widget refreshes, so changes in the date or time zone may appear with a delay.

Configuration checks. Configuration checks retain up to 24 recent refresh reports in the local App Group container. They contain group, layout-version, and configuration identifiers; action names and matching status; grid, widget size, and refresh time. Reports do not contain SystemShortcut or other system action objects, are not uploaded to the developer, and are excluded from exported backups. They describe name matching at a past refresh, not whether a widget is still on the Home Screen. Multiple widgets with identical configurations share a report. These records are not an inventory of individual Home Screen widgets. Older reports are evicted when new reports exceed the limit. Deleting a group removes its saved layout but does not immediately clear its past check reports.

Network requests. Icon searches send search terms, app names or store IDs, and any storefront parameter used by the request to Apple's iTunes Search / Lookup endpoint (itunes.apple.com). Artwork is downloaded from URLs returned by that endpoint, usually on Apple's mzstatic.com domain. Standard network connections disclose connection information such as an IP address to the server. AppFolder does not additionally send advertising identifiers, an account, or precise location. Apple's handling of its services is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Website links. Website names, complete HTTP/HTTPS links including query parameters and fragments, and each website’s opening preference are stored in local groups. The app preference in Website opening settings stays on this device and applies only to websites explicitly set to Follow app settings. Existing websites without a saved preference, and new websites, still use the system default unless you change their preference. Changing the app preference does not rewrite saved URLs, access the network, or check installed browsers. Typing or editing a URL, choosing an opening preference, adding or saving a link, previewing a layout, receiving a URL in the share extension, and importing a backup do not automatically visit the website or download an icon. Test Link and website tiles open the complete link using your chosen method. The system handles the default method; a default website tile on the Home Screen opens without routing through AppFolder. Tiles set to Browse in app, Chrome, Firefox, or Follow app settings first open AppFolder, which hands the link to the selected browsing method. Follow app settings uses this device’s current app preference when opening. In-app browsing uses a system-provided Safari modal page that can load website resources and execute scripts. AppFolder does not read that Safari page’s content, passwords, or browsing history. Websites and browsers handle data under their own policies. If the selected browser cannot open, the app shows a message rather than automatically switching browsers; you can explicitly choose the system default. Website names, links, per-website opening preferences (including the choice to follow app settings), and saved icons are included in group files you choose to export or share. The device’s app preference is not exported. These data are not sent to the developer.

Fetching website information. Only when you explicitly tap Fetch Website Information in the website editor does AppFolder request the entered website, validated HTTP/HTTPS redirect destinations, and icon servers or CDNs declared by that website. If no usable icon is found, it tries that site’s /favicon.ico. This metadata retrieval feature reads a limited amount of HTML to extract names and icon URLs; it does not save webpage body content in groups. Metadata retrieval does not use a third-party favicon lookup service, render webpages, execute scripts, or use your browser’s signed-in state. It does not persist cookies, webpage response caches, or login credentials. Website and icon servers receive the requested URL, including any query parameters, and normal connection information such as your IP address, and handle requests under their own policies. This information is not uploaded to the developer. Retrieved names and icons appear as suggestions first; they become a local website name and separate icon only after you choose to apply them and save the group. Retrieval does not rewrite your saved destination URL using redirects or a webpage’s declared canonical URL.

Contact tiles. You can enter a name and number manually or select a contact and one phone number in the system contact picker. AppFolder receives only the final selected name and number; it does not read your entire address book, contact photos, or persistent contact identifiers. The name, single number, chosen operation, and custom icon are stored in the local group, do not follow address-book updates, and are not uploaded to the developer. Selecting, previewing, saving, and importing do not initiate contact. Tapping a tile opens AppFolder, which reads the current record before asking the system to handle a call or message. Phone calls require system confirmation, and messages are not sent automatically. FaceTime requires another tap on Start inside the app; queued, expired, or restored requests do not run automatically. Names, numbers, operations, and related artwork are included in group files you choose to export or share.

Shared website inbox. Only after you choose AppFolder in the system share sheet and tap Save does the extension store the full URL (including query parameters and fragments), an optional title you enter, a record identifier, and a timestamp in WebShareInbox in the local App Group container, with a limit of 100 records. Receiving a shared URL does not read shared text or the clipboard, retrieve webpage content, extract titles, icons, or other metadata, make network requests, or automatically launch the main app. During the add-to-group flow, the app automatically removes the corresponding pending record only after you confirm and successfully save the group; cancelling the addition or a failed save retains it. You can also swipe left on a record in Pending websites to remove it manually and undo the most recent removal on the current page. Removing a pending record does not delete content from saved groups. Pending records not yet added to a group are excluded from backups and Share Group files.

Preview. The sample preview uses local illustrations. It does not read your Home Screen, notifications, wallpaper, or installed-app list, and it makes no network requests.

Permissions. AppFolder does not request access to your whole address book, calendars, location, microphone, camera, or notifications, and it does not enumerate installed apps. Images for action icons, contact icons, website icons, and folder covers can be imported through the system photo or file picker. The app reads only the images you select, without requesting access to your whole photo library. When you choose iCloud Drive or another cloud location, the system or the relevant file service provider may download the file. AppFolder does not save the original file, its source URL, or persistent access authorization. Images are cropped and re-encoded as small icons on your device without original photo metadata such as location, and are not uploaded to the developer. You choose actions in the system's Edit Widget panel.

Backup files. You can choose to export a backup containing group layouts, contact names, single phone numbers and chosen operations, website names, full links and opening preferences (from format version 13, with Follow app settings added in version 14), standard and dark folder covers, custom icons (including contact icons, website icons, photo thumbnails, and date-style settings), and manual icon matches with their selected storefront countries. Photo covers, contact icons, and website icons are included; an identical image used in multiple places is stored once. Share Group creates a local temporary file containing only the selected saved group and its related artwork, matches, and action-library records. You choose the recipient or save location in the system share sheet. The current export format is version 16; versions 1–16 can be read. Backups do not include system actions, Home Screen widget configurations, search-region preferences, the device’s app website opening preference, downloaded artwork caches, configuration check reports, or diagnostic logs. Import reads only the file you choose and adds group copies and missing icon settings after confirmation, preserving existing custom icons and manual matches. Import does not restore selected system actions; you configure actions again in the system widget panel. Websites do not require system actions. Older backups without an opening preference use the system default; explicitly saved system-default choices also remain unchanged. Import preserves each website’s choice to follow app settings, which uses the destination device’s app preference when opened later. Importing does not open websites, access the network, or rewrite saved URLs. AppFolder does not encrypt the backup file. It is saved to the location you select in the system file panel. If you choose iCloud Drive or another cloud location, that service provider handles the file; it is not sent to the developer. Deleting data in the app does not delete exported copies, which you can manage or delete in Files.

Retention and deletion. Removing a record in the action library and saving your changes clears that name's library tags and archive state without deleting groups or icons. The name can remain in the list if another source still provides it. Group layouts, artwork, and matching records stay on your device for future display. You can delete a group in My Groups to remove its saved layout. To remove a folder's custom cover settings, open Edit cover in its menu, set standard to Contents preview and dark to Use standard cover, then save the group. You can also delete the folder or group. A website’s menu lets you edit or delete its link, or restore its default icon; these changes apply after saving the group. Artwork assets still used by another group, current edit history, or delete-undo history are retained; unused assets are removed during later local cleanup. Reset all to automatic in Icon Studio removes the manual match and both standard and dark custom icons for that action name, rather than all downloaded artwork. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it while keeping its data, removes its local data. iOS manages cleanup of shared containers.

App Store reports. The developer may view aggregate download or diagnostic reports supplied by Apple. AppFolder includes no additional analytics or crash-reporting service.

Children and changes. AppFolder is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's information. Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date.
